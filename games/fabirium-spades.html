About this game

📺 iPad in the middle

  • The iPad is the table: played cards (each turned towards its player), bids, tricks and scores.
  • Each phone shows only that player's cards. Tap Show table to see the table on your phone too.
  • The music plays on the iPad only.

📱 Phones only

  • No iPad needed. Whoever taps Phones only becomes the host.
  • Every phone shows the whole table plus its own cards.
  • The music plays on every phone; each player can turn it off.

2 vs 2 or solo

Partners sit across from each other, or play 1 v 1 v 1 v 1.

Bots fill empty seats

Only 2 or 3 of you? Computer players take the empty seats.

Flying cards

Cards fly from your phone to the table, and won tricks fly back into your pile.

Never lose your seat

If a phone locks or reloads, you're back in the same seat with the same cards.

Fair quitting

The game only ends early when every player agrees.

Music and sounds

Relaxed background music and card sounds, with a switch for each.

Start a game at home

The game runs on a computer at home. Everyone joins from the same Wi-Fi.

On a Windows PC

  1. Install Node.js (the free LTS version). Only once.
  2. Download the game, right-click the zip and choose Extract All.
  3. Double-click Start Fabirium Spades.bat. The first start needs internet once.
  4. If Windows Firewall asks, tick Private networks and click Allow access.
  5. Open the address it shows (like http://192.168.0.102:3000) on the iPad and tap iPad in the middle, or on a phone and tap Phones only.

Good to know

  • Mac or Linux: run npm install, then npm start in the folder.
  • Same Wi-Fi: the iPad and every phone must be on the computer's Wi-Fi. Keep the computer on while you play.
  • Mobile data: needs the online version, coming soon.
  • Phone died? Tap that player's name on the table and a bot plays until they're back.

How to play

A game, step by step

  1. Join: scan the QR code on the table (or type the 4-letter code), enter your name and pick a seat.
  2. Set up: choose 2 vs 2 or 1 v 1 v 1 v 1, the target score, Nil and the bag penalty, then Start game.
  3. Bid how many tricks you'll win. 0 is Nil.
  4. Play: tap a card to lift it, tap again to play. Grey cards can't be played.
  5. Tap Next hand after each hand. First to the target wins.

The rules

  • Spades are always trump. Follow the led suit if you can.
  • Nobody can lead spades until one has been played, unless they hold only spades.
  • Bid made: 10 per trick bid, +1 per extra trick (a “bag”). Missed: −10 per trick bid.
  • Nil: +100 if you win no tricks, −100 if you win any. Every 10 bags costs 100.
