A chat-style sales calculator for shops. Each salesman types the prices and taps Complete sale, and every sale is saved under his name. The owner sees the statistics, cash, leaderboard and reports.

Screenshots

About this app

The app is in Bangla by default, with an English switch.

For salesmen

  • Type a price and tap ↑ to add it. Use × for more than one.
  • Tap Complete sale (বিক্রি সম্পন্ন). The bill appears like a chat message.
  • Choose Cash or QR, and name items or the customer if you like.
  • Print a receipt on the shop's Bluetooth printer (Android).
  • Undo your own sale within 5 minutes if no receipt was printed. After that, ask the owner with a reason.

For the owner

  • Statistics for today, 7 days, 30 days or any date: sales, customers, average bill, sales by hour.
  • Cash: morning cash in the drawer, cash out with tags, and Close the day with any cash difference shown.
  • Approve void requests, or void a sale yourself with a reason.
  • Leaderboard, daily to monthly reports printed as strips, and staff by Gmail.

Nothing gets lost

Every sale is kept for good. Mistakes get a correcting record instead of being deleted.

Checked on the server

The undo and void rules are checked on Google's servers, not only in the app.

Bangla first

Natural Bangla, with an English switch. Receipts print in English.

Getting started

Install

  1. Android: download the APK, open it, allow installing from this source, then tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway.
  2. iPhone: open the web app in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. New versions show an Update message inside the app. Printing works only from the Android app.

Every day

  1. Sign in with Google, using the Gmail the owner added.
  2. Owner, morning: in Cash (ক্যাশ), type the drawer cash and tap Put in drawer.
  3. All day: salesmen record each sale and tap Complete sale.
  4. Owner, closing: tap Close the day, then check Statistics (পরিসংখ্যান).

Keep every sale in one place.

Free for Android and iPhone.
