About us

Why “Fabirium”?

Farhana + Abir + ium

The name is made from our two names, plus “-ium”, the ending of an element's name. So we drew it as a new element: symbol Fa, number 3, for our three branches: Arcade, Labs and Cuts.

We build things we want to use ourselves: games to play with family and friends, apps for real shops we know, and videos about it all.
