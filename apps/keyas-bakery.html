The shop app for Keya's Bakery. Show customers the cake catalog, take orders, keep track of the fridge, and record every taka the salesman collects and hands over.

Screenshots

About this app

The app is in Bangla by default. Tap EN at the top for English.

Cake catalog

Nearly a hundred designs in groups (classic, heart shape, two tiers) to show customers.

Orders

From the catalog or the customer's own photo: flavour, pounds, price, advance and due, from new to delivered.

Fridge

Every cake with its price. Put new ones in, tap a picture to sell, and see the fridge history for any day.

Cash account

Cash with the salesman, today's sales and money given to the owner. Every handover is recorded.

Reports

Daily, weekly and monthly totals, including who sold what.

Receipts

Print on a Bluetooth printer from the Android app: one copy for the customer, one for the shop.

How to use it

Everyday tasks

  1. Take an order: open Gallery (গ্যালারি), tap a design, then Order this cake and Confirm order.
  2. Sell from the fridge: open Fridge (ফ্রিজ), tap the cake's picture, then Sell.
  3. Hand money to the owner: in Cash, tap Give money to owner.
  4. Owner: add people in Settings → New user, stock the fridge with Add cakes to fridge, and check Reports (রিপোর্ট).

Install

  1. Android: download the APK, open it, allow installing from this source, then tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway.
  2. iPhone: open the web app in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. New versions show an Update message inside the app. Printing works only from the Android app.
